★ Product Definition & Value Proposition

Buildloom is a commercial high-performance Web Animation & Shader Engine containing 282 curated interactive component labs (3D Canvas, Physics Simulators, WebGL Shaders, GSAP ScrollTrigger, Lenis Smooth Scroll, and Micro-interactions). It equips web design agencies, frontend developers, and SaaS teams with drop-in, studio-grade visual effects.
